About Galico

Galico Home Comfort Inc. is a licensed gas fitting and heating contractor based in Almonte, Ontario. We specialize in water heater installation, tankless water heaters, gas line installation, and residential heating systems. Serving homeowners in Almonte, Kanata, Stittsville, Carleton Place, Arnprior, Perth, and the surrounding Ottawa Valley, Galico delivers professional gas fitting and heating services with a focus on safety, reliability, and 5-star customer service.
