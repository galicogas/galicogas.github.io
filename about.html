About Us

Galico was founded on a simple belief: work should be done with purpose, ownership, and pride.

Based in Almonte, Ontario, Galico is a licensed gas company specializing in natural gas and liquid propane systems for residential homes. We focus on water heater installation and replacement, gas fitting, appliance connections, and hydronic heating — delivering safe, code-compliant, and professionally executed installations across Ottawa West, Arnprior, Carleton Place, Perth, Smiths Falls, and the surrounding communities.

But Galico is not just another contractor.

Built for the Long Term

Most companies are built to be sold.
Galico is being built to last.

Our vision is to create a company that will serve homeowners for generations — a 200-year legacy rooted in craftsmanship, accountability, and community impact. That means every installation is completed as if our name will still be attached to it decades from now — because it will be.

At Galico, work is not just about completing a job — it is about doing it right.

Gas and hydronic systems demand precision. They demand respect for safety codes. They demand technical discipline. We specialize in this work because it matters. When you invite someone into your home to work on gas appliances or heating systems, you are placing trust in them. We take that responsibility seriously.

Every project — whether it’s a tankless water heater installation, a gas range hookup, or a radiant floor heating system — is completed with long-term reliability in mind.

Family Comes First

We believe success in business should strengthen families, not strain them.

Galico is structured around the principle that professional excellence and personal life must coexist. When technicians and partners operate with stability and purpose, homeowners receive better service. That balance is part of our foundation.

Community Impact

We are proud to serve Almonte and the greater Ottawa West region. These are not just service areas — they are communities we live in and contribute to.

As Galico grows, so does our commitment to:

  • Developing skilled trades professionals
  • Supporting local initiatives
  • Building partnerships with home builders
  • Raising the standard of gas and hydronic work in the region

Our goal is not simply to complete installations — it is to elevate the standard of service homeowners expect.

Service Guarantee

Professional Installation Guarantee

At Galico, every installation is performed to manufacturer specifications, Ontario gas and safety codes, and industry best practices. We stand behind our workmanship and installation standards so homeowners can feel confident that their system is installed safely and correctly from day one.

Transparent Pricing Commitment

We provide clear, upfront pricing before work begins. No hidden installation fees, unexpected add-ons, or last-minute pricing changes — just straightforward recommendations and professional service.

Licensed & Code-Compliant Work

All gas-related work is completed by licensed gasfitters, with proper testing, verification, and commissioning performed before the system is placed into operation. Safety and compliance are never optional steps — they are part of every installation.

Manufacturer Warranty Protection

We install equipment according to manufacturer requirements so your product warranty remains fully valid. When applicable, we assist homeowners with warranty documentation and registration to ensure long-term protection.

Clean & Respectful Jobsite Promise

Your home is treated with care throughout the installation process. Work areas are protected, old equipment is removed properly, and the installation area is left clean and ready for normal use.
